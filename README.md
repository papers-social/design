# [papers.social](http://papers.social) Design Documentation Repository

The service would consist of two sites (links to domains):

- [papers.social](http://papers.social)
- [papers.forum](http://papers.forum)

The first site, [papers.social](./papers_social/), would be the main site. It
will host a feed that shows papers that are of interest to the user. Each paper
in the feed would have a method to access the paper, a way for people to mark
it as a paper they liked and would like to share, and a way to to review the
paper. More details [here ./papers_social/](./papers_social/).

The second site, [papers.forum](./papers_forum/), would be an additional site.
It will be a forum where people can discuss more freely about specific papers,
areas of research, or any topic that may be of relevance to the discussion of
papers. More details [here ./papers_forum/](./papers_forum).
